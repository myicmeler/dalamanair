About this data.
  1. Weights are read directly from the Eksponering tab of each fund in the Nordnet app. Only the positions visible on screen are included — twelve per fund, covering 65,8 % (Borea), 68,2 % (Eika) and 69,6 % (Pensum) of each portfolio. The remainder is shown as Not disclosed on screen and is treated as unknown, not as cash.
  2. Pensum listed Sparebanken Norge twice (7,4 % and 3,5 %) and SpareBank 1 Østfold Akershus twice (2,3 % and 2,2 %), almost certainly two instrument classes of the same issuer. Those pairs are merged here and marked in the table.
  3. Business type and home market are my classification for comparison purposes. Nordea is treated as Finnish and NOBA as Swedish by domicile.
  4. Live prices. On load the page fetches delayed quotes for each holding from Yahoo Finance and marks each fund to today's move on a look-through basis: the fund's value is adjusted by the weighted change of the shares it holds that were priced. Only the disclosed, priced portion moves — undisclosed and unpriced holdings are held flat, so the estimate is conservative. Fetching runs through a public CORS proxy and can fail or rate-limit; when it does, the page falls back to the static weights and says so. These are single-moment weights, not fund NAVs, and nothing here is investment advice.
